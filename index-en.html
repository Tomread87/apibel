About Us

Apibel was born from Alessandro Teruggi's passion for beekeeping and nature. We are a small artisan business based in Invorio, dedicated with care and respect to looking after our bees. Our goal is to bring to your table a product that is honest, natural, and reflects the authenticity of handcrafted work.

The Territory

Northern Novara

"Our bees forage in the unspoilt woods and meadows around Invorio, the "Gateway to Vergante", in Fontaneto d'Agogna, the "green heart of the Novara plain", and in Moncrestese, an "ancient village embraced by woodland". A land rich in biodiversity, sheltered from the winds and warmed by the Piedmont sun, giving our honey its distinctive aromas and flavours."

Production Method

We extract our honey cold by centrifugation, preserving all of its nutritional and enzymatic properties. It undergoes no heat pasteurisation treatment: what you taste is exactly what the bees created.

  • ✓ Cold extraction
  • ✓ Unpasteurised
  • ✓ Respect for bee welfare
